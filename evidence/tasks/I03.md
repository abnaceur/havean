# I03 — listing workflow service

Implemented one API state transition service for submission, review, publication, rejection, pause, sold, leased, expiry and archival. The service locks the listing, checks actor assignment, current version and the transition map, and writes state, immutable status history, audit and outbox within one transaction. HTTP transitions also require an idempotency key.

Publication requires verified facts and authority, a positive exact-decimal stored price, complete unit facts, an eligible organization agent and approved photographs. Rental publication checks active occupancy. A moderator cannot approve or reject a listing they created, own or are assigned. Owner request approval uses this service and attaches its approved photographs to listing media.

Authors can submit drafts in the consumer and operations workbenches. Moderator status forms require a reason and offer verification confirmation. Actions with no allowed transition are hidden.
