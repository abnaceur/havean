# F11 — Representative deterministic English fixtures

`packages/test-support/src/fixtures.ts` generates the canonical `fixtures/beijing.json` with a fixed fixture clock and stable UUIDs. Database seeding consumes this file; it does not scrape benchmark listings or people. Persisted fixture domains have reviewed migrations.

Inventory: 60 resale, 30 rental and 10 commercial listings plus two explicit edge listings; eight developments, 24 floor plans, six districts/communities, four agents and eleven role personas across five organizations. Edge cases cover an absent price, sold inventory, missing photography, an active lease and a foreign organization's private draft. Plans do not label illustrative photographs as actual drawings. Media fixtures and source/rights records are under `packages/test-support/assets`.

`tests/unit/fixtures.test.ts` verifies factory output generated twice is identical to the stored JSON, IDs are unique and stable, counts/edge states are present, visible fixture text is English and listing authoring/money schema checks pass. Independent clean database fingerprints cover the persisted seed, including private scope and lease fixtures (F10 evidence).

Evidence: `evidence/fixture-reset-first.json`, `evidence/fixture-reset-second.json`, `evidence/isolated-reset-first.log` and `evidence/isolated-reset-second.log`. The fixture tests passed in the second reset unit suite (16 assertions total across fixture, business and observability tests). F10 clean-reset verification is complete.
