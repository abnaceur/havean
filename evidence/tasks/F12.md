# F12 — Quarantine, validated variants and private documents

Uploads start with an owned intent and a ten-minute encrypted ticket scoped to actor and asset. Binary finalization checks ticket, exact MIME and byte size, quarantine state and rights; writes the original into the authenticated `haven-private` bucket; scans through pinned ClamAV INSTREAM; then decodes/re-encodes images or videos. Documents reject active content/encryption/embedded files. Resource publication review is distinct from successful scanning. Originals and variants have no public bucket policy.

Images: Sharp enforces the 40-million-pixel cap, rotates pixels, creates WebP display (at most 2000×1600 for standard images) and 480px thumbnails, and omits EXIF/XMP. Panoramas require 2:1 equirectangular geometry. Video: pinned FFmpeg validates/transcodes actual decodable input and produces a poster. Approved property videos use an authorized byte-range endpoint with 206/416 handling.

Private documents require an authorized owner/moderator session; signed download tickets bind actor/asset and expire after sixty seconds. Download audit events persist. Anonymous or unrelated users cannot access originals/documents; scanned but unpublished property media remains private.

Evidence: desktop browser assertions in `evidence/isolated-reset-second.log` passed:

- `media-security.spec.ts`: real JPEG with private EXIF becomes correctly sized display and thumbnail WebP without EXIF; unauthenticated S3 reads of the actual original and thumbnail return 403; anonymous unreviewed display 404; forged ticket 400.
- `rich-media.spec.ts`: actual videos, drawings and linked panoramas upload, moderate and render; byte-range response checks; actual ClamAV EICAR rejection, fake video decoder rejection and oversized intents denied.
- `journeys.spec.ts`: spoofed image denied, private PDF downloaded by its owner, unrelated user's document request 404 after moderator review.

Fixtures are synthetic and carry provenance/rights records. These checks certify the local media contract, not benchmark visual parity or production inventory rights.
