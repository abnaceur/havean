# Agency invitations and membership authority

Identity owns invitations and retained membership history. An invitation is restricted to an actual active manager's selected agency; client organization IDs never select another agency. Verified recipients see only invitations matching their current active profile email. Acceptance grants exactly the invited agent or manager permission, after locking the current invitation and checking its version, pending state and seven-day expiry. A recipient may consent to membership in another agency; managers cannot manage another agency's invitations or memberships.

Membership mutations check actual manager membership, target organization, version, permitted role/status, active target profile and reason. Managers cannot modify their own authority. Database guards protect direct writes; narrow owner functions permit only verified invitation acceptance/decline and the first consumer membership after a verified OIDC callback. Permissions are reread from active memberships on every authenticated request. Removal therefore denies the same existing session immediately.

History records actual actor, target, agency, previous/current role/status, version, reason and timestamp, alongside audit and outbox in the transaction. Audit identifiers survive account lifecycle changes; private reasons are omitted from own privacy exports. History cannot be edited by the application role. Invitations use the durable verified recipient inbox; no email delivery is claimed.

Staff permissions require actual ACR 2. Existing mandatory staff OTP remains required. Consumers who enroll an authenticator can authenticate at level 2 before consenting to a staff invitation. Unenrolled consumers retain ordinary password registration/recovery. This uses Keycloak's conditional configured authenticator and level-of-authentication flows: https://www.keycloak.org/docs/25.0.6/server_admin/ . Dedicated browser fixtures exercise actual consumer realm roles, configured OTP, verified callback, invitation acceptance and immediate removal with one session.
